foidfinder

Privacy

Applies to the foidfinder.net website and the foidfinder browser extension. Last updated 13 November 2026.

Your images stay on your computer

foidfinder identifies images with a model that runs in your browser, on your own GPU. The images you check are never uploaded to foidfinder or anyone else, and the results are not sent anywhere.

What foidfinder collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no cookies.

Network requests

  • The model. On first use your browser downloads the model (about 178 MB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co and its content delivery network). Hugging Face receives the request, including your IP address, as with any download. Its privacy policy is at huggingface.co/privacy.
  • The website. foidfinder.net is served through Cloudflare, which processes requests to deliver the site. foidfinder keeps no access logs.
  • Images, in the extension. When you right-click an image and choose "Identify with foidfinder", the extension downloads that image from the site that hosts it, as your browser did when it showed the image, sending the page's address (origin only) as the referrer. If the image can't be downloaded, the extension takes a screenshot of the visible tab, crops the image out of it, identifies it locally and discards it.

What is stored on your device

The model is kept in your browser's storage so it downloads only once. Clear the site's data for foidfinder.net, or remove the extension, to delete it.

Extension permissions

The extension can access all sites so it can download the image you right-click from wherever it is hosted. It does nothing on a page until you choose its menu item there: it injects no scripts in the background and reads no page content other than the image you picked.
