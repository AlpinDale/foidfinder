foidfinder

Full-quality downloads

By default foidfinder only reaches the tab you use it in. Images hosted on another site, or protected against hotlinking (pixiv, for example), are then cropped from a screenshot of the page, at the size they are shown, which can lower accuracy.

Allowing access to all sites lets foidfinder download the original image from wherever it is hosted. It only does so for the image you right-click, and still does nothing on a page until you use its menu item. Images are identified on your computer and never uploaded.
